The rules

Flavor only. Nobody gets a 10.

Instagram tells you what looks good. This guide tells you what tastes good. Every spot gets judged the same way.

  1. Taste is the score

    The number is about one thing: how the food tastes.

  2. Looks don't count

    Decor, plating, the strip mall parking lot, the service. None of it moves the number.

  3. Nobody gets a 10

    Scores run 0 to 10 with decimals. A 10 doesn't exist, which keeps every other number honest.

  4. Price matters a little

    Every review notes the price and whether it's worth it. It's context, not the headline.

  5. Local first

    No huge international chains. This guide is for the spots you'd never find by scrolling.
